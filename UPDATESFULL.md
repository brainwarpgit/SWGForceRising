### Committed vehicle recovery fix

- Traced a race between deferred database restoration and login's forced vehicle storage. `SceneObject::notifyLoadFromDatabase()` captured the saved zone and queued an insertion. Login's `unloadSpawnedChildren(true)` could store the vehicle first, clearing its zone, linked owner, and datapad status. The queued task then unconditionally inserted that stored vehicle into the captured zone, leaving a visible vehicle without the owner link required by mount and radial store actions.
- The deferred insertion task now rechecks the object's current local zone and quadtree/octree membership while holding the object lock. It skips a stale restoration after storage or a zone change, and skips duplicate insertion after another path has already inserted the object. Normal restoration still waits for zone managers and permits saved parented objects. No persistence schema, vehicle templates, configuration switches, or ownership permissions changed.
- Existing affected vehicles whose datapad control device still points to the vehicle can recover through the existing forced-storage step at login; calling the stored vehicle recreates both owner and control-device links. This does not invent ownership or recover missing/deleted database references. No live data was modified, and the assistant did not build or run Core3.
